Who I am

Technology and business, working together

I am Muoch Gatluak — a graduate with an academic background in Information and Communication Technology and Business Management. My work sits where those two disciplines meet: understanding the problem, deciding what the data says, and then building something that actually solves it.

On the technical side I build websites and work with databases. On the business side I plan, analyse and organise — turning rough ideas into structured plans, clear reports and services people are willing to pay for. In between, I write and design, because a solution nobody understands is not finished.

Alongside that, I write and publish. My upcoming book, Being Young Is No Shame, is dedicated to encouraging young people who are facing difficult circumstances — a reminder that struggle is part of the journey, not a verdict on it.
