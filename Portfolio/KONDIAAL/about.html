Who We Are

KONDIAAL ENTERPRISE CO. LTD is a successful multi-business services providing company with far-reaching proficiency in a number of customer-tailored services in the field of Money Transfer Services, Money Exchange, General Trading (Supplies) & Construction and lots more.

Incorporated under the laws of The Republic of South Sudan, Kondiaal has undertaken many challenging projects which have made it accumulate extensive experience and skill in procurement, costing/pricing, project management, administration and clientele relations all which have made it an ideal partner in all its business scopes.

Our main objective is to provide our clients with an "I am assured" experience when selected to execute any project. Our success is exceptionally linked to our professional, technical and mechanical capacity in executing our commitments and to our team of highly committed and skilled members.

Kondiaal is a multi-supply and General trading Company legally registered with the purpose of supplying quality logistics and services to all the potential clients across South Sudan. It is one of the most reliable suppliers in the region with the capacity of delivery the quality logistics and services beyond expectation.
